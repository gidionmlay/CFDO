# Why the design works and what CFDO should carry forward

## Design logic

**OBSERVED:** The eye goes first to the massive hero type and image, then to the blue expertise statement, then to repeated large headings. Tiny labels and rules act as orientation rather than competing headlines. This creates a clear primary/secondary/tertiary hierarchy.

**INFERRED:** The architectural image treatment, measured grid, and blue/neutral contrast communicate precision and premium expertise. The expansive blank space around headings gives each chapter its own pause. The accordion condenses several services without making the page a grid of equal cards. Projects supply concrete proof; quote and news then vary the rhythm. Motion mainly introduces content and reinforces chapter transitions; it does not need to be reproduced exactly to preserve the narrative.

For CFDO the emotional center should shift from technical certainty to credible community presence. The brief's dual identity requires a warm institutional opening and a deliberate transition to professional consultancy. The reference's linear story is valuable; its engineering visual identity is not.

## Inspiration map

| Reference finding | Decision | CFDO direction |
|---|---|---|
| Strong opening statement and clear first action | PRESERVE | Lead with CFDO identity, mission, and participation choices |
| Chapter sequence: positioning → capability → method → proof → updates | PRESERVE | Reorder around CFDO's Discover → Participate journey |
| Oversized editorial headings | ADAPT | Fluid accessible type with CFDO voice and less extreme wrapping |
| Split introduction and service hierarchy | ADAPT | Balance programmes and consultancy without implying one is secondary |
| Four-stage process story | ADAPT | Listen → Design → Deliver → Sustain from brief; validate wording with CFDO |
| Project gallery | REINTERPRET | Evidence-led programme stories once real records exist |
| Accordion mechanics | REINTERPRET | Use for dense consultancy detail; programmes may need a more human editorial grid |
| Blue technical duotone, crosshair graphic, architectural photography | REPLACE | Original community/environment imagery and CFDO identity |
| OCI brand, names, copy, quotes, photos, logos | REPLACE | Original or approved CFDO content only |
| Unverified impact totals or invented project/testimonial entries | AVOID | Truthful placeholders or conditional omission |
| Motion that delays access to essential content | AVOID | Content remains available with reduced motion and on slower devices |

This is a design and engineering boundary, not a legal assessment.
